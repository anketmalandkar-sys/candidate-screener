SUMMARY
Fourteen years building transaction systems. I tend to be the person who gets

EXPERIENCE

Principal Engineer, Calderwood Financial (2016–present)

Rewrote the settlement engine that moves roughly £2bn a month. The old system
was a nightly batch; the replacement is event-driven and settles continuously.
I wrote most of it myself, in the language we standardised on for backend work —
I have been writing it since the 2.x days and still reach for it first.

I designed the relational store underneath it. Getting the isolation levels
right took three attempts; the write-ahead log tuning and the partitioning
scheme are mine, and month-end reconciliation dropped from six hours to twenty
minutes as a result.

On the operational side, I built out our container orchestration layer and the
declarative infrastructure tooling that provisions it. Before that, everything
was hand-configured boxes and a wiki page of tribal knowledge. Now a new
environment is one command and about nine minutes.

I also designed the resource-oriented HTTP interface that our partner banks
integrate against — twelve institutions on it now, and we have not had to
version-break it once in four years.

Earlier: Senior Engineer, Thameside Software (2011–2016). Payments integrations
and a lot of legacy modernisation.

WHAT I CARE ABOUT
Correctness under load, systems that page nobody at 3am, and writing things down
so the next person does not have to reverse-engineer my reasoning.
